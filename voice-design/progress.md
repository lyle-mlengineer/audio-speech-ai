## Notes / decisions (hand-written, preserved by the updater)

- **LoRA, not full FT.** ~10 h of audio across ~120 speakers is enough to move the
  design space toward Kenyan voices, but far below the volume needed to add a new
  language. LoRA (LM + DiT, r=32) keeps the 2B base model's Swahili and English
  intact while re-centring voice design on Kenyan speech.
- **`sample_rate: 16000` is mandatory.** It is asserted against
  `audio_vae.sample_rate` (AudioVAE encoder input, hop 640 -> 25 lat/s).
  `out_sample_rate: 48000` is the decoder and only affects TensorBoard audio.
- **Checkpoints live on Drive**: `artifacts/checkpoints/<run>/step_%07d` plus a
  `latest/` copy. `train_voxcpm_finetune.py` auto-resumes from `latest`, so a
  disconnect costs at most `save_interval` steps (default 100).
- **A `--max-hours` guard** stops training gracefully just before the Colab
  session limit; the trainer's SIGINT handler writes a checkpoint first.
- **Speaker-level captions.** Gender/age come from dataset metadata, pitch and pace
  are *measured* (`audio_features.median_f0`), and all of a speaker's clips share
  one description. Per-clip captions would teach the model noise: the same voice
  labelled "low-pitched" and "bright".
- **Speaker-disjoint validation.** Val speakers are never trained on, so val loss
  measures whether the caption generalises to a new Kenyan speaker rather than
  whether the model memorised a voice.
- **Caption dropout (~10%).** Some rows keep a plain transcript, so the adapter
  still works without a description and the base model's behaviour survives.
- **`ref_audio` stays off.** Voice design is caption-only; reference conditioning
  would let the model lean on the reference instead of the description.
- **Overfitting is the main failure mode.** Watch `loss/diff` and listen at each
  saved step; prefer the checkpoint that still follows new text over the one that
  replays training audio (FAQ issue #169).
